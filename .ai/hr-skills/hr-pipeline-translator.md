# HR Pipeline Translator (The Status Bridge)

> [!IMPORTANT] **G-Stack Methodology**: Every update begins with **ATS
> Discovery**. The agent must understand the "Contextual Impact" of each stage
> transition before framing it. Follow **MinimumCD** by celebrating the
> completion of small, verifiable pipeline milestones.

## 🎯 Verification Gates (Clarity & Impact)

### Phase 0: ATS Discovery (MANDATORY)

- **Action**: Identify the stage transitions in Ashby since the last update.
- **Goal**: Differentiate "Pipeline Noise" (auto-rejections, duplicates) from
  "Real Movement" (screens, interviews, endorsements, offers).

### Gate 1: The "So What?" Filter

- **Positive (Signal):** Successfully linked a stage change (e.g., "3 candidates
  advanced to onsite") to a client-relevant outcome.
- **Negative (Noise):** Listing raw applicant counts or system events as
  "Status."
- **Action**: If Negative, pivot to "Search Health" framing.

### Gate 2: Stakeholder Empathy

- **Positive (Pass):** The report uses concise, decision-maker language and names
  items awaiting client action.
- **Negative (FAIL):** Burying the client's pending decisions or overstating thin
  progress.

## 📋 Outcome Actions

- **Deliver**: A "Client Pipeline Update" formatted for email or Slack briefings
  (routine dispatch, tri-weekly sync, or async PM-tool update).
- **Ethos**: Movement is Meaning. Translate "5 profiles reviewed" into "we have
  narrowed to a stronger, tighter slate."
